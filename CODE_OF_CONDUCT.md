# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient and constructive — many contributors are students.
- Give and gracefully accept feedback; assume good intent.
- Focus on what is best for the community and for safe, defensive research.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, trolling or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- **Project-specific:** contributing payloads that contain real harmful instructions, real
  credentials or personal data, or that target systems the contributor does not own or is not
  authorized to test. Payloads must stay synthetic (see `payloads/README.md`).

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers using the contact method listed on the
repository (for example GitHub's private reporting features). All reports are reviewed promptly
and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate, following the
Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
